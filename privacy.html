Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Data Autopost uses

When you connect an account, the platform gives Autopost an access token. For TikTok, Autopost also receives your public display name, which it shows so you can confirm the right account is connected. Autopost requests only these permissions: user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.

2. Where data is stored

Tokens are stored only on the user's own computer, in a file that only that user can read. Autopost keeps a local log of past uploads: the platform, the date and the link to each post.

3. Sharing

Autopost has no server. It does not collect, sell or share any data. Your videos and their captions go directly from your computer to the platform you chose.

4. Passwords

Autopost never asks for, sees or stores your password. You sign in only on the platform's official login page.

5. Deleting your data

To remove everything, delete the local token files and revoke Autopost's access in your TikTok, YouTube or Instagram settings.

Politique de confidentialité (FR)

Autopost n'a pas de serveur. Les jetons de connexion restent uniquement sur l'ordinateur de l'utilisateur. Aucun mot de passe n'est demandé ni stocké. Aucune donnée n'est collectée, vendue ou partagée. Les vidéos partent directement de l'ordinateur vers la plateforme choisie.

Pour tout effacer : supprimer les fichiers de jetons locaux et retirer l'accès d'Autopost dans les réglages de chaque plateforme.
